# BACT IT Complete Website

## What it is, in two lines
A complete, responsive BACT IT website presenting its technology services, training, and study-abroad guidance.
It helps businesses, learners, and students explore relevant offerings and send enquiries on web or mobile.

## Who it's for
Government and public organizations, enterprises, healthcare and education providers, retailers, IT and telecom businesses, SMEs and startups, people seeking IT training, and students exploring study abroad.

## Core features and experience
- A professional, mobile-friendly experience using deep blue (#0B2345), orange (#FF8A32), and light backgrounds.
- Straightforward navigation and pages for Home, About, Industries, Training & Courses, Study Abroad, Projects/Portfolio, and Contact.
- Separate Software Solutions, ICT Infrastructure, Cybersecurity, AI Solutions, IT Training, Study Abroad, and Digital Marketing service pages.
- The seven service areas and subcategories from the brief, plus the “Why choose BACT IT?” differentiators.
- Course and study-abroad information with enquiry paths; detailed schedules, syllabi, and admissions content only where supplied.
- Contact details using info@bactit.com and www.bactit.com, with a form for name, organization, email, phone, service of interest, and message.
- Portfolio structure without fabricated projects, client claims, or endorsements.

## Phase 1: what gets built now
Build the complete public website and enquiry flow with responsive web and mobile layouts, all page areas and service categories described in the brief, and the supplied BACT IT copy and contact details. Keep the flagship product area unpopulated because no product details were provided. Do not invent course schedules, study-abroad guarantees, portfolio evidence, or endorsements. No CRM, client portal, paid registration, or external integrations are specified for this phase.
